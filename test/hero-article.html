Town Libraries Become Busy Civic Hubs

Reading rooms that people once avoided have been turned into places for culture, coworking and caregiving.
Civic Engagement
Courtesy of the Town Library Trust
September 25, 2026 3 min read

As Sam prepared for a job interview with an overseas firm, he worried that the power would fail halfway through, as it often did at home. Then he remembered the library down the road, with its reliable internet and quiet rooms.

Five years ago the same library was a place people avoided: the roof leaked, the shelves were half empty and the reading room was dark. Today it is full from opening time, with students, job seekers and parents with young children.

The change came from a small trust that persuaded the council to hand over the building, raised money for repairs and asked residents what they wanted from it. The answer was simple: somewhere to work, learn and meet.

Other towns are now copying the idea, and the trust says three more libraries will reopen next year, each run with a committee of people who use it.
