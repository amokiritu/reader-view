A Beginner's Guide to the Home Garden

Watering without waste

Deep, infrequent watering encourages roots to grow downward, where the soil stays cooler and moister. A light sprinkle every evening does the opposite, keeping roots near the surface where they dry out quickly on hot days.

Water early in the morning when possible, aiming at the base of plants rather than the leaves. A layer of mulch such as straw or shredded leaves slows evaporation and keeps weeds from competing for moisture.

Planning for next year

As the season winds down, look back at your sketch and notes. Rotate crop families to different beds next year to reduce pests and diseases that build up in the soil, and plant a cover crop in any bed that would otherwise sit bare over winter.

A garden is never really finished. Each year adds a little knowledge, and each season the plan on paper gets a little closer to the garden you imagined at the start.
