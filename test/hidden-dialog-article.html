Harbour ferry returns after ten years

The harbour ferry made its first crossing in a decade on Monday, carrying two hundred passengers and a brass band across the bay in a little under twenty minutes.

The service was withdrawn when the old pier was condemned. A new landing stage, paid for by the port authority, opened last week after two years of building work.

Boats will run every half hour on weekdays, the operator said, and hourly at weekends until the summer timetable begins.
