Council approves the new bridge

The city council voted on Tuesday to approve the new river bridge. The council met for four hours before the vote, hearing from engineers, shopkeepers and a delegation of rowers who use the river every morning before work.

Engineers expect the span to carry twelve thousand vehicles a day once the approach roads are finished, the transport office said in its filing.
